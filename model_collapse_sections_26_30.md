## 30. Confirmatory H1 on test seeds 1001–1300 (FROZEN 2026-11-10, before any test-seed run)

**Decision rule.** Per κ ∈ {0.6, 0.9}: equivalence is claimed only if both ℓ_τ and ε^tot reject their non-equivalence nulls (intersection–union; no multiplicity adjustment needed). Overall H1 is supported only if the claim holds at both κ = 0.6 and 0.9. At κ = 0.3 no primary IU claim is made. A failed TOST is reported as non-equivalence or inconclusive, not as evidence of equivalence; margins are never widened; no endpoint or κ is added after the run.
**Interpretation limits.** H1 is a negative control that N and H are the same law implemented two ways (coupling identity I1); it makes no claim that noise improves anything. Stability, distributional quality and ancestry stay separate. Test seeds 1001–1300 are used here for H1 only.
